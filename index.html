    How ordering works

    1. 1

      Choose your cakes

      Tap a cake, pick how many and add it to your basket. Switch between the parve and dairy lists any time.

    2. 2

      Check your basket

      Open "Your order" to change quantities, add your name and any request of your own.

    3. 3

      Send it by email

      Your email app opens with the whole order filled in. Tap Send and it reaches us.
